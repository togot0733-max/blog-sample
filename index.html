Emergency readiness

🔥 Wildfire evacuation protocol +

Identify two exit routes. Keep go-bags with N95 masks, water, and documents. Sign up for local alerts — defensible space saves homes.

🌊 Flood safety — never drive through water +

6 inches of moving water can sweep a car. Move to higher ground immediately. Avoid contact with floodwater — it can contain sewage and chemicals.

🌡️ Extreme heat action plan +

Stay hydrated, avoid peak sun, check on elderly neighbors. Cooling centers save lives — know the nearest one.
