Emergency protocols

Protocol 01

Activate SIM-PIN Lock

The Problem

Scammers don't need your physical phone. They use leaked black-market database details to execute a remote "SIM-Swap" through corrupt carrier agents — letting them intercept your banking OTP codes on their own devices.

The Fix

Set a mandatory hardware SIM PIN immediately.

Settings ➔ Security & Privacy ➔ More Security Settings ➔ SIM Card Lock

Toggle Lock SIM Card to ON, enter the carrier default (usually 0000 or 1234), then change it to a private 4-digit PIN.

Once active, your SIM becomes un-clonable — it cannot be mounted on any external device without your hardware passkey.

Protocol 02

Purge Bank App Cache

The Problem

Banking apps save a digital footprint called a "Session Token" in background memory so you don't retype your password constantly. Black-market exploits use background web scripts to silently clone these signatures while you browse.

The Fix

Hard-purge your local device tokens every 14 days.

Settings ➔ Apps ➔ App Management ➔ [Banking App] ➔ Storage Usage

Tap Clear Cache, then Clear Data. This terminates all active background session keys and forces hidden remote trackers to lose access instantly.

Protocol 03

Emergency USSD Freeze

The Problem

If you notice a sudden drop in cellular signal (a sign of a live SIM-swap attempt) or an unexpected bank debit alert — waiting to call support is a critical error. The response window is under 3 minutes.

The Fix

Memorize or physically write your bank's offline USSD Emergency Account Lock shortcode on a card in your wallet.

Dialing the freeze string from any active phone line instantly places an administrative "Post No Debit" (PND) lock on your entire profile.

Keep it mapped out so you can isolate your assets within seconds — without needing internet.
